How to Play Color Linez

Objective

Arrange five or more balls of the same color in a straight line to clear them and earn points.

Instructions

  1. Click a colored ball to select it.
  2. Click an empty cell to move the selected ball.
  3. The ball moves only if a clear path is available.
  4. Three new balls appear after a successful move.
  5. Create matching lines to clear balls and earn points.

Game Rules

  • The board contains 9 rows and 9 columns.
  • There are seven possible ball colors.
  • Lines can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.
  • At least five balls of the same color are needed.
  • Each cleared ball earns one point.

Tips

Plan your moves carefully, group balls of the same color, and keep empty spaces available.
