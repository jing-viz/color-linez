Welcome to Color Linez!

Color Linez is a simple puzzle game. Your goal is to match 5 or more balls of the same color in a line to make them disappear and earn points.

Instructions

  1. The game board has 9 rows and 9 columns.
  2. Colored balls appear in some of the squares.
  3. Balls come in different colors, such as blue, pink, green, and yellow.
  4. Empty squares are spaces where you can move balls.
  5. Choose a colored ball you want to move.
  6. Click the ball once to select it.
  7. Click an empty square where you want to move the ball.
  8. The ball will move if a clear path is available.
  9. You cannot move a ball through squares occupied by other balls.
  10. Try to create a line of 5 or more balls of the same color.
  11. You can make lines horizontally (left to right), vertically (up and down), or diagonally.
  12. When you create a matching line of at least 5 balls, those balls disappear.
  13. You earn 1 point for each ball that disappears.
  14. For example, matching 5 balls earns you 5 points.
  15. After each successful move, 3 new colored balls appear in empty squares.
  16. New balls make the game more challenging.
  17. Try to clear matching balls before the board becomes too crowded.
  18. Plan your moves carefully and aim for the highest score!

Remember!

Match 5 or more balls of the same color to earn points.

The more balls you clear, the higher your score!
