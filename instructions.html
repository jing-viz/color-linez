color-linez is a browser-based matching game powered by Javascript, wherein the goal is to match 5 colors of game pieces in a line and obtain a high score.

How to play:

  1. Double click anywhere on the game board. This will spawn three pieces anywhere on the board, with their colors chosen at random.
  2. Select any piece on the board. Clicking on an empty space on the board with a piece selected will attempt to move the piece to that spot.
  3. New pieces will be added to the board each time a piece is moved.
  4. A piece can be moved anywhere on the board, regardless of distance, provided there is a clear path for it to take.
    • Attempting to move a piece to a spot that is blocked by other pieces will fail.
  5. Line pieces of the same color together. They can be lined horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  6. When five or more pieces are lined up, they are removed from the board. The number of pieces removed is added to the player's score.
  7. The game ends when the board is filled with pieces and no more pieces can be moved.

Hints:

  1. Each movement will spawn the same number of pieces regardless of distance. Get the most distance out of each move you can.
  2. Connect shorter lines of pieces together to remove as many as possible in one go.
  3. There are seven different colors of pieces. Keep control of all of them, rather than focusing on a single color.
